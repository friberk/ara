# AI Research Assistant

User-approved brief: a single-user academic research workspace for CS59300 homework. Researchers search papers, save them, upload PDFs, read grounded summaries and ask document-specific questions. Success is five working end-to-end workflows with retained source/page provenance, reproducible Docker setup and authentic development reflection.

Web SPA for desktop and mobile. Frequent tasks are searching, selecting, reading and comparing a response to its source. The first viewport is the actual search workbench, not marketing. Missing metadata/fulltext, processing, provider failure and interrupted generation are essential states.

Pinned design from the approved plan: quiet light surfaces, dark text, one restrained accent, system fonts, content-first lists, narrow metadata column and broad reading pane; mobile stacks/sheets. No invented claims or synthetic data in normal operation. Interface copy explains user actions and recovery, not internal engineering.

Scope: React/Vite/strictTS/Tailwind4/shadcn, Query+ReactRouter, RHF/Zod. No auth, payments, GPU prerequisite or landing page. Document requirements in docs/implementation-plan.md. Development deadline October10; five working flows and reflection take priority over advanced enhancements.
